Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

A magnificent white marble mausoleum and one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

2. Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

The famous Palace of Breeze constructed with red and pink sandstone.

3. Backwaters, Kerala

A network of serene brackish lagoons and lakes parallel to the Arabian Sea coast.

4. Golden Temple, Amritsar

The holiest gurdwara of Sikhism, famous for its full golden dome and spiritual peace.

5. Gateway of India, Mumbai

An iconic 20th-century monument located overlooking the Arabian Sea.
